Put your .mp4 video files in this folder.

Example: reel1.mp4, reel2.mp4, documentary1.mp4

Then open index.html in a text editor, find the "projects" list near the bottom,
and for each project change:

    videoSrc: ""

to:

    videoSrc: "videos/reel1.mp4"

(using the actual filename you put in this folder)
